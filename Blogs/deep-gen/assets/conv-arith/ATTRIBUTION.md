# Third-party images: conv_arithmetic

The `.gif` animations in this directory are taken from:

  Vincent Dumoulin and Francesco Visin,
  "A guide to convolution arithmetic for deep learning", arXiv:1603.07285
  https://github.com/vdumoulin/conv_arithmetic

They are redistributed here under the MIT License (see LICENSE.txt), which the
authors explicitly extend to the images: "The code and the images of this
tutorial are free to use as regulated by the licence and subject to proper
attribution."

Attribution is given inline in the figure captions of Blogs/deep-gen/ch-05.html
and links back to the source repository.

Files:
  no_padding_no_strides.gif                 convolution, no padding, unit stride
  padding_strides.gif                       convolution, padding, stride 2
  no_padding_no_strides_transposed.gif      transposed conv, no padding, unit stride
  no_padding_strides_transposed.gif         transposed conv, no padding, stride 2
  padding_strides_transposed.gif            transposed conv, padding, stride 2
  padding_strides_odd_transposed.gif        transposed conv, odd padding/stride
